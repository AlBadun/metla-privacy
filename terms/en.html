4. What METLA does and does not promise

METLA finds junk — thumbnails, temporary and incomplete downloads, installers, old and large files — as well as exact duplicates and empty folders; it shows what apps have access to (Anti-Spy), how often they run, and the state of the device (Device Pulse). Before deleting, METLA shows what will be deleted, and the decision is yours.

What METLA cannot do or does not promise:

  • Android limits. Android does not let any app clear other apps’ cache — METLA shows its size and opens the settings. METLA can ask Android to stop another app’s background work only on Android 8–13. On Android 8–9 Radar only asks Android to stop the background work of restricted apps; on Android 10–13 it notices their background work and asks to stop it; on Android 14 and above it only notices and tells you.
  • Results depend on the device, the Android version and what is stored on it. We do not promise a specific amount of freed space, speed or battery life.
  • Space is freed when files leave Quarantine, not at the moment of cleaning.
  • Anti-Spy is not an antivirus. It shows which data apps have access to, but it does not look for malware and does not protect against it.

5. Your files and Quarantine

Files you delete in METLA go to Quarantine first. A file is deleted permanently when you confirm it or when the period you chose ends (30 days by default); when Quarantine runs out of space, METLA warns you and asks before deleting the oldest files. A file can be restored while it is in Quarantine and METLA has file access; on Android 8–9 — only to the folders you chose.

On Android 11 and above Quarantine is the hidden .METLA_Trash folder in shared storage: file managers and apps with All files access can see it; it remains after METLA is uninstalled, and after reinstalling METLA does not see the earlier files in it — you can delete them with a file manager. On Android 8–10 Quarantine is kept inside METLA and is erased together with it, and also by “Clear storage” in Android settings.

Keep important files somewhere else as well. METLA does not copy your files off the device and does not make an Android backup.
