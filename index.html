Privacy Policy

Last updated:

1. Introduction

Welcome to METLA Cleaner. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Android application.

2. Information We Collect

METLA Cleaner does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All scanning and cleaning operations are performed locally on your device. No logs, analytics, or usage statistics are sent to external servers.

3. Permissions Used

The app requests the following Android permissions strictly for its core functionality:

  • QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES – to identify installed apps for cache analysis
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – to locate and remove temporary files
  • INTERNET – only for checking app updates (optional)

Permissions are used solely on-device and never shared.

4. Third-Party Services

METLA Cleaner does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking libraries. Your data never leaves your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not target users under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Policy Updates

We may update this policy if app functionality changes. The updated version will always be available at this URL.
