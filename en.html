Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to METLA Cleaner ("we", "our", "the app"). We build a premium tool for cleaning and optimizing Android devices, and your privacy is our top priority.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. Please read this document carefully.

Core principle: Sensitive information about your device — such as your list of installed apps, app usage statistics, and the permissions other apps have requested — is processed entirely on your device and is never transmitted anywhere. Some other, limited information is sent to our service providers to keep the app reliable and to support paid subscriptions, as described in sections 2 and 3 below.

2. Information processed locally on your device

To power its core cleaning and Anti-Spy features, METLA Cleaner reads the following directly on your device. This information is used only to power these specific features and never leaves your device:

  • File system data: the app scans folders and files to find junk, duplicates, temporary files, and cache. Scan results are shown only to you and are never sent anywhere.
  • List of installed apps: we read the names of apps installed on your device to identify ones that are rarely used or safe to remove.
  • App usage statistics: with the Usage Access permission you grant, we check how often and for how long you use each app, to flag ones draining your battery or storage, and to power Sleep Mode.
  • Permissions of other installed apps (Anti-Spy): we check which sensitive permissions — camera, microphone, contacts, SMS, location — other installed apps have requested, so we can warn you about risky ones.

3. Information sent to our service providers

Some parts of the app rely on Google Firebase, a set of cloud services we use to keep the app reliable and to support paid subscriptions. This means limited information does leave your device for the specific purposes below. We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising or tracking.

  • Anonymized analytics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics): standard, anonymized information such as device model, OS version, and approximate location, used only to find bugs and improve app stability.
  • Account and subscription data (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Functions, Cloud Firestore): if you purchase a subscription or join a Family plan, we store the minimum information needed to verify your purchase and manage family membership — an anonymous account identifier, your Google Play purchase token (used only to confirm your subscription with Google, never to track you elsewhere), and, if you choose to link a Google account, the associated email address (used only to restore your access if you reinstall the app). If you use Family sharing, we store the membership status of invited members so the app knows who has access.
  • Purchases (Google Play Billing): all purchases are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details.

4. Permissions requested by the app

Here is what each permission the app requests is actually used for:

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): required for deep folder scanning, finding duplicates, removing junk, and restoring files from Quarantine.
  • Photos, videos, audio (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): to scan and clean your media files (Android 13 and above).
  • Installed apps list (QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES): to power the cleaning and Anti-Spy features described in section 2.
  • Usage stats access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): to display time spent in apps and power Sleep Mode; granted separately in system settings.
  • Uninstall apps (REQUEST_DELETE_PACKAGES): lets you uninstall an app directly from METLA Cleaner.
  • Stop background apps (KILL_BACKGROUND_PROCESSES): lets you stop a running app from the Apps screen.
  • Notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used for notifications about scheduled auto-clean results and reminders.
  • Network status (ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used by the Device Pulse screen to show your current network status.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH, BLUETOOTH_CONNECT): used by the Device Pulse screen to show whether Bluetooth is connected.
  • Precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION): used only so Android will reveal the name of your connected Wi-Fi network on the Device Pulse screen — this is a requirement of Android itself, not something we use for tracking. We do not read, store, or transmit your actual location.
  • Biometrics — fingerprint or face (USE_BIOMETRIC, USE_FINGERPRINT): used for the Zero-Tap feature — confirming your identity when registering and using a sign-in key between your devices. The biometric check itself is performed by Android's own secure system — we never receive or store your biometric data.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION): added automatically by the Firebase Analytics library we use (section 3). We do not show ads and do not use this identifier to build advertising profiles.
  • Internet access (INTERNET): required for the Firebase and Google Play Billing services described in section 3, and to load this Privacy Policy page.

All permissions are used strictly for their stated purpose.

5. Quarantine and cleaning history

The Quarantine feature temporarily stores deleted files in the app's internal storage, allowing you to restore them if needed. All Quarantine data and Cleaning History are stored only on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.

6. Data retention and account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from Profile → Account & data → Delete account in the app, or by emailing us at metla.support@gmail.com. This permanently removes your account data from our servers. One exception: we keep a record of your purchase token to prevent subscription fraud, as required for anti-fraud purposes — by that point it no longer contains any information that identifies you. Deleting your account does not cancel a Google Play subscription; that must be cancelled separately in Google Play.

7. Children's privacy

METLA Cleaner is not intended for use by persons under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

8. Security

Locally stored data is protected by your device's own security, such as your screen lock and disk encryption. Information sent to our service providers (section 3) is protected using Google's standard security practices.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app functionality or applicable law. All changes will be published on this page with a new "Last updated" date above. We recommend checking this document periodically.
