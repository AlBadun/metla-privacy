Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 31, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to METLA Cleaner ("we", "our", "the app"). We build a premium tool for cleaning and optimizing Android devices, and your privacy is our top priority.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information when you use our app. Please read this document carefully.

Core principle: METLA Cleaner is designed to operate entirely locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data, files, or device information to any external servers.

2. What we do NOT collect

To be completely clear, here is what METLA Cleaner never does:

  • Does not collect personal data (name, email, phone, address).
  • Does not scan or upload your files, photos, videos, or documents to the cloud.
  • Does not analyze your behavior for advertising or marketing purposes.
  • Does not share data with third parties, including ad networks or analytics services.
  • Does not use trackers, cookies, or any other tracking technologies.

3. Information processed locally

To perform its functions, METLA Cleaner processes the following information exclusively on your device:

  • File system data: the app scans folders and files to find junk, duplicates, temporary files, and cache. Scan results are shown only to you and are never sent anywhere.
  • Installed app list: used for cache cleaning and app activity analysis. This information never leaves your device.
  • App usage statistics: time spent in apps (only with your granted permission). Used locally for the Sleep Mode feature.

4. Permissions requested by the app

For full functionality, METLA Cleaner requests the following Android permissions:

  • All files access (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE): required for deep folder scanning, finding duplicates, removing junk, and restoring files from Quarantine. Without this permission, some cleaning features will be unavailable.
  • Usage stats access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): allows displaying time spent in apps and managing background processes.
  • Notification permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): used only for notifications when auto-clean completes (if enabled).
  • Internet access (INTERNET): used exclusively to load this Privacy Policy document.

All permissions are used strictly for their stated purpose and are not used for data collection.

5. Quarantine and cleaning history

The Quarantine feature temporarily stores deleted files in the app's internal storage, allowing you to restore them if needed. All Quarantine data and Cleaning History are stored only on your device and can be deleted by you at any time.

6. Children's privacy

METLA Cleaner is not intended for use by persons under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

7. Third-party services

METLA Cleaner does not use third-party analytics systems, advertising SDKs, trackers, or any other libraries that could transmit data outside your device. The app is fully self-contained and autonomous.

8. Security

Since all data is processed locally, there is no risk of data leakage via the internet. We recommend using your device's standard security measures (screen lock, encryption) to protect information stored on it.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in app functionality or applicable law. All changes will be published on this page. We recommend checking this document periodically.
